Product Reservation System

Products can be reserved by customers for a maximum period of
15 minutes.

When a product is reserved, the inventory quantity is temporarily
held for that customer.

Other customers cannot purchase the reserved quantity during the
reservation period.

If the customer completes the payment within 15 minutes, the
reservation is converted into an order.

If payment is not completed within 15 minutes, the reservation
expires and the inventory becomes available again.

A customer can reserve multiple products, but each reservation
must have a unique reservation ID.

The reservation service uses Redis to maintain temporary
reservation information.

The product database uses PostgreSQL as the primary persistent
database.

Kafka is used to publish reservation events to downstream services.
